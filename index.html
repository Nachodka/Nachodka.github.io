My approach

My approach

My role is to help you understand yourself more clearly and explore choices that fit your needs and circumstances. You don’t have to find the right answer straight away.

In our sessions, we can explore your feelings, needs, and boundaries, and consider how this understanding might support you in everyday life.

  • A thoughtful, collaborative approach
  • Transactional Analysis
  • Individual online sessions
  • Regular professional supervision
How I work

How I work

I offer space to talk about what feels difficult, confusing, or important to you. Together, we can explore your emotions, relationships, and recurring patterns, at a pace we discuss along the way.

My approach draws on Transactional Analysis, which offers a way to understand how we relate to ourselves and others. We might explore familiar patterns in your relationships, messages you have learned about yourself, and how these influence your choices today.

My academic training in cognitive psychology also informs my work: how we pay attention, interpret experiences, remember events, and make decisions. I draw on research while remaining attentive to your individual experience.

What we can explore together

01

Relationship with yourself

  • Self-criticism

    When you’re hard on yourself and find it difficult to rest or allow yourself to make mistakes.

  • Perfectionism

    When high expectations leave you exhausted or make it difficult to begin.

  • Self-understanding and self-acceptance

    When you would like to understand your feelings, reactions, and wishes more clearly, and relate to yourself with greater kindness.

02

Emotions and how you’re feeling

  • Emotions and needs

    When it is difficult to identify what you feel, what you need, or where your boundaries are.

  • Shame, guilt, and fear

    When these feelings become overwhelming or make it difficult to decide what to do.

  • Feeling stuck

    When you feel unsure where to go next and would like space to explore your options.

03

Relationships, conflict, and change

  • Breakups and endings

    When a relationship has ended, or is changing, and you’re finding it difficult to adjust or let go.

  • Conflict and communication

    When you would like to understand what happens in your relationships and explore ways to express yourself, respond to others, and set boundaries.

  • Life changes

    When familiar things are changing and you would like support in understanding what you need next.

Getting started

Getting started

You don’t need to prepare or find the perfect words to explain what brings you here. A short first message is enough — we can work out the next steps together.

  2. 02

    We arrange a time

    I’ll answer your questions, explain how sessions work, and we’ll arrange a suitable time to meet online.

  3. 03

    Your first session

    We’ll begin with what brought you here and what you would like support with. You can choose how much you feel ready to share.
