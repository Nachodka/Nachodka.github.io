Approach

Relationship with yourself

  • Excessive self-criticism

    When your inner critic makes it hard to rest, make mistakes, and support yourself.

  • Perfectionism

    When “it has to be perfect” drains your energy and delays important steps.

  • Self-understanding and self-acceptance

    When you want to see your reactions and desires more clearly and treat yourself more gently.

02

Emotions and inner states

  • Emotions and needs

    When it is hard to name what you feel, what you want, and where your boundary lies.

  • Shame, guilt, fear

    When these emotions create a sense of deadlock and block alternatives.

  • Feeling stuck

    When it matters to see that a situation can have more than one scenario.

03

Relationships, conflict, and change

  • Ending relationships

    When you need to move through a breakup, unfinished business, or emotional attachment.

  • Conflict and communication

  • Life changes

    When you need to choose a direction, lean on yourself, and start moving toward a new state.

How we begin

Three calm steps to your first session.

You do not need to prepare or phrase your request perfectly. A short first message is enough — we will take the rest together.

  1. 01

    Write to me

    Send a brief message through any channel that works for you. You can describe what matters right now in a few words, or simply write “I would like to book a session.”

  2. 02

    We choose format and time

    I will reply and help you orient yourself, and we will find a convenient time for an online meeting.

  3. 03

    First online session

    We meet online at a calm pace. Without pressure or expectations — we start where you feel comfortable.
